Statements print like a printer

Jack: "the order and the color determine the appearance of the output", and you can keep layering. These previews simulate that with 80 mock Credits. Try the buttons.

1 · Print order (arrangement vote)

Today every pooled Statement prints in deposit order, which is effectively random. With the arrangement vote, anyone proposes an order and depositors adopt one with more than 40 of 80 slots. It freezes before the sale, so buyers see exactly what prints.

2 · Custom batches

Anyone opens a batch with its own rules, alongside the open queue. Rules are fixed at the first deposit and checked on-chain for every Credit. The creator gets no special powers.

Open a batch
Opening costs one of your own Credits as the first deposit. No extra fee.

3 · Backed auctions (sell first, burn second)

Nothing burns until a sale is locked in. A batch must be backed (a whale commits ETH for the whole batch, any amount) before its auction can start; the best backing is the opening bid. If the auction ends below the depositors' minimum, they have 24 hours to accept the best bid by majority (41 of 80 slots), or everyone is refunded and the Credits stay unburned.

4 · Layering (back a Statement with Credits)

A layer batch prints 80 more Credits onto an existing Statement (the canvas). The more layers, the darker the print; every layer stays on-chain. Layers burn only when a buyer or backer is locked in.
